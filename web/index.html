Pad settings

Hold Home+M for 3 s to put your pad in config mode, then Connect.

Link tests

Write sizes: sends the most data a command takes, in 20- and 96-byte writes, as settings for a target that does not exist (nothing is staged), and checks the pad parsed all of it. Updater round trip: restarts the pad into its updater, connects again without the chooser, reads it, and reboots it back into its app (the link ends).
